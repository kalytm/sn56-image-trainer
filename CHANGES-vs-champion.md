| D1 | `trainer/godtrainer/selection.py` (new); `engine.py` (`holdout_score`, `evaluate_candidates`, `_select`, delta-average, member/soup acceptance via `Trainer._better`, `_base_eval`, `HoldoutAborted`); `train.py` (`--select-rule`, `--select-k`, `--base-eval-frac`, validation); `scripts/image_trainer.py` (`COMMON`, Ideogram extras) | Holdout uses 4 seeded noises per band (the champion used 1, Flux 2; the official evaluator uses 16, so 4 is a variance-reduction compromise, not a match). Every candidate keeps its paired per-image vector under a member-qualified tag (`m1:ema@300`), so members never share a key. With ≥3 holdout images a candidate replaces the best only if its paired gain exceeds k·SE across images (k=1, a heuristic); otherwise a smoother/later state (deltaavg > ema > raw > base) wins only if its per-image mean is not worse. With <3 images the champion's lower-mean rule applies. Paired mode always scores arithmetic means: `train.py` rejects other `--select-metric` values with it, and Ideogram's p75 was dropped. Deadline guard: the identity fallback is saved before any scoring. Every holdout evaluation runs against a cutoff (the task deadline minus the save reserve, and inside a member also the member's budget): it refuses to start when the cutoff has passed or the last measured cost cannot fit, projects the remaining cost before every forward batch, and discards a result that completes late. An aborted candidate scores NaN (never replaces), later candidates at that point are skipped, and the projected cost becomes the new estimate. The base evaluation aims for ≤15% of the remaining budget by reducing the noise count (fixed for the rest of the run, estimate rescaled); at one noise it may use the whole scoring budget, so 15% is not a hard cap. `--select-rule min` reproduces the champion's selection. | implemented; codex 4c009d0 findings addressed; CPU tests pass; **no GPU A/B yet**. `retrain_all` (inherited, disabled) is outside the paired rule. |
| D2 | `engine.py` (`crossfit_folds`, `_crossfit_ok`, `_run_crossfit`, `_global_select`; member base tags; `_base_eval(cutoff=)`); `train.py` (`--crossfit`, `--crossfit-min-images`, `--crossfit-min-member-s`, `--crossfit-eval-max`, validation) | Two-fold cross-fit: the task images are split deterministically into folds A/B; member 0 trains on A and selects its step (D1, paired against its own fold base) on ≤`crossfit_eval_max` images of B, member 1 the reverse; member budgets are the remaining time divided by the members left (member 0 gets half; member 1 gets what remains). The shipped weights are the exact average of both members' selected deltas, exported by `save` → `lora_state_dict` (up factors weighted ½ and concatenated, scale kept via alpha; direct deltas averaged). There is no per-task acceptance test of the soup and no cross-member score comparison (members are scored on different images); member out-of-fold minima are logged as selection-biased diagnostics. A member whose best is its base is not averaged in (the other is shipped alone); member 0's best is saved as a fallback before member 1 trains. Due evaluations that cannot finish are skipped; the horizon shrinks (never grows) when progress falls behind, so the deadline always wins. Step time is the full iteration (case/noise preparation through the EMA update, after a CUDA sync), excluding evaluation. The 35% cap covers in-loop evaluation points only, not the tail or base evaluation. Replaces fixed `--eval-every` and the fixed 2.2–3.3× evaluation reserve. `--schedule time` reproduces the champion. | items 1 and 3 implemented; CPU tests pass, incl. engine-level fake-clock tests of the real holdout/selection/`train_member` control flow; `epoch_cap` is 5 in the shipped recipe since D6 (fitted on 3 training-only dev tasks, weak evidence); **no GPU A/B yet**. | D5 | `trainer/godtrainer/assets.py` (`safetensors_keys`, `FLUX_ARTIFACTS`, `Assets.flux_standalone`, `Assets.flux_artifact`; FLUX branches of `diffusion_sd`, `text_encoder_sds`, `vae`) | The champion can only train FLUX models shipped as one bundled checkpoint (diffusion model + VAE + text encoders). | D6 | `engine.py` (`run` member loop, `_extra_members_ok`, `_extra_member_fits`); `train.py` (`--extra-members`, `--extra-member-min-frac`, validation); `scripts/image_trainer.py` (`COMMON`: `--epoch-cap 5 --eval-grid-gamma 1.0 --extra-members 1 --eval-count 6`) | Dev evidence (training-only dev suite, phases A/B): with a time-bound cosine over ~8 passes, the selected state came at 35–50% of the horizon (z-image ema@902/1002 of ~2400–3090 steps, flux ema@1184 of 2334, ideogram4 ema@401/959). After that the holdout loss rises slowly while the LR is still high. The cosine is now capped at 5 passes over the image×band case cycle (D3 `epoch_cap`), so the LR anneals by the time the states were best. If, after member 0, the time left is at least 0.8× its wall time (eligibility is this time test with a positive cap configured; it does not require that the cap actually bound) (after the save and soup reserves), one more member is trained from a new seed with the same cap and all the remaining time. Member 1 replaces member 0 only under the D1 paired rule; the exact average of both (rank-concatenated, as in the champion's soup) is scored once and kept only if it wins under the same rule. Evaluation points are spread uniformly (gamma 1) instead of tail-dense, 6 per member. Member 0 gets the whole budget (no soup reserve until a second member is known); a fixed `--soup N` keeps the champion's even split. | implemented; CPU tests pass; `epoch_cap` 5 and min-frac 0.8 come from 3 dev tasks (weak evidence) and are tested in B2. |
| D7 | `engine.py` (`_forward_backward`, `ckpt_full`) | Memory fallback. The champion's Ideogram4 recipe turns gradient checkpointing off (`--no-ckpt`) for speed, which runs at the 80 GB limit: in B2 (H100 80 GB) dev task 04ac2174 (latents 47×64) trained, but ea66240a (48×64, 768×1024 images) ran out of memory at step 1 for the champion and for ours, and both produced only the untrained identity LoRA. Now, when a training step runs out of GPU memory while some blocks are not checkpointed, the step's partial gradients are dropped, every transformer block is checkpointed (stride 1) and the step is retried once; a second out-of-memory, or one with all blocks already checkpointed, is raised as before. The retry starts only if 1.5× the larger of the failed attempt and the current step estimate, plus the reserve the loop keeps after a step (D3 tail or the time schedule's evaluation reserve; none for fixed-step retraining), fits the member cutoff; otherwise the member ends on its best state with no update. After a retry the D3 schedule drops its earlier (faster-mode) step samples, takes the iteration's preparation plus the retried step (excluding the failed attempt and recovery) as the new estimate and replans at once (the horizon still only shrinks); the time schedule's step estimate is reset. Checkpointing stays on for later members. Tasks that fit keep the fast path unchanged. The trigger is a runtime memory error, never task identity. Recorded in `summary.json` as `memory_fallback`. | implemented; codex 432f455 and 464af3a findings addressed; CPU tests pass (control flow with a stand-in exception, incl. the real `train_member` loop); **GPU check pending** (rerun ea66240a; it does not show that the retry fits in memory). |
